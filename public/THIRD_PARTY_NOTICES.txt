Sound garden ice palette - Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
--------------------------------------------------------
Twelve original compositions and synthesized loops by Boundstoy contributors.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
No external recordings or sampled instruments. The rejected cloth candidate
is not part of this palette. Strudel was used only as an external authoring

Per-file render evidence, source patches and mastering:

Explosion and buildup listening candidates - Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sixteen original synthesized sound files by Boundstoy contributors (2026),
forming eight buildup/explosion pairs. No external recordings, samples,
instrument banks or third-party compositions.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The softened airy-puff-blast.wav is the approved gameplay explosion.
Other files remain listening alternatives; the Airy puff buildup is not selected.
Credits and reproducible source: audio/explosion-candidates/LICENSE.txt.
Per-file measurements and hashes: audio/explosion-candidates/manifest.json.
The offline generator and listening-page code remain MIT licensed.

Other-sounds comparison board - Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Sixteen original seeded synthesized sound designs by Boundstoy contributors
(2026), in audio/sound-board/. No external recordings or sampled instruments.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
death-feather.wav (Falling feather) and ui-tick.wav (Soft tick) are approved
gameplay sounds. The remaining fourteen files, including original warm
checkpoint and triumphant exit jingles, remain listening suggestions.
Other current game cues and existing ice files are referenced unchanged,
under the CC0 licenses above. Source and credits: audio/sound-board/LICENSE.txt.
Measurements and hashes: audio/sound-board/manifest.json.
The offline generator and board code remain MIT licensed.
