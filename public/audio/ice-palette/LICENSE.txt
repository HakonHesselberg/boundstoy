Boundstoy Sound garden ice palette (2026)
=======================================

All twelve WAV assets and their editable .strudel.js compositions/patches:
Original compositions and synthesis by Boundstoy contributors.
Dedicated to CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Weightless pad, Warm organ, Single ribbon, Airy musical veil, Hollow flute,
Hushed choir, Velvet strings, Distant glow, Floating fifths, Glass halo,
Soft shimmer and Warm reed.

No external recordings, instrument/choir samples, film music, Kenney assets,
wind/noise beds or the rejected Silk glide recording are used.

Generated with the real @strudel/web@1.3.0 offline audio renderer.
Strudel's AGPL-3.0-or-later engine is an external authoring tool, not included
in this folder or the game. https://codeberg.org/uzu/strudel
The engine license does not license other people's compositions or samples;
these twelve compositions are original.

Weightless and Airy musical veil use the retained original candidate captures.
The ten other sounds use new original patches. Full 28-second captures remain
in the task's authoring artifacts. *.render.json records engine/browser and
source hashes. Native reverb initialization is asynchronous; raw re-renders
are not promised byte-identical. The shipped masters are fixed local files.

Processing: select a phase-aligned 6-10-second steady-state period after the
initial attack; one-second cosine overlap; static EBU R128 loudness matching
to -30 LUFS; stereo 48 kHz PCM16 export. No repeating onset/release envelope.
manifest.json records actual lengths, levels, correlations and hashes.

Authoring and playback code retain the project's MIT license.
These are alternatives for human listening, not an accepted final palette.
