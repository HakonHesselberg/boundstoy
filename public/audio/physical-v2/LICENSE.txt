Boundstoy Physical v2 sound palette
=================================

All WAVs in this folder are offered under Creative Commons Zero 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

step/sound.wav and mud/sound.wav:
Recordings by Fantozzi, edited into individual footsteps by qubodup.
Originals: Fantozzi-StoneL1.flac and Fantozzi-SandL2.flac.
https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
https://freesound.org/people/Fantozzi/packs/10338/
The source pack explicitly identifies these recordings as CC0.

bump/sound.wav and ui/sound.wav:
Recordings by rubberduck, from "100 CC0 SFX".
Originals: hit_01.ogg and switch_01.ogg.
https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx
The source pack is explicitly CC0.

soft, hard, warning, blast, death, checkpoint and win:
Original deterministic sound synthesis by Boundstoy contributors (2026).
The generated WAVs are dedicated to CC0 1.0. The generator source code remains
under the project's MIT license.

Changes to external recordings: mono/32 kHz conversion, rumble filtering,
high-frequency filtering, light compression, duration editing, endpoint fades
and peak mastering.
No endorsement by the original creators is implied.

Provenance, source hashes and reproduction: docs/AUDIO.md and
scripts/prepare-audio.ts. No Kenney material is included.
