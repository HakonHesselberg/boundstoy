Boundstoy other-sounds board

The sixteen WAV files in this directory are original Boundstoy sound designs
by Boundstoy contributors (2026), dedicated to CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No external recordings, samples, instrument banks or third-party compositions
are used in these designs. They are original seeded offline synthesis, including
the original checkpoint/exit note sequences.
death-feather.wav and ui-tick.wav are approved for gameplay. The other fourteen
files remain listening suggestions, not gameplay selections.

Editable source: data/sound-board.ts and scripts/prepare-sound-board.ts.
Rebuild with Node 24+ and FFmpeg: node scripts/prepare-sound-board.ts
The manifest records source hashes, asset hashes and per-role measurements.
Measurements use fixed original physical-palette references and gains, not
previously generated candidates. They include gameplay attack/release in six
events, at least 1.2 seconds apart (longer for jingles; see each group's
authoringReference). These are not isolated short-clip integrated loudness.

Other current game cues, approved Airy puff and existing ice choices are
referenced without copying or modifying their files. All are also CC0; see:
  ../physical-v2/LICENSE.txt
  ../explosion-candidates/LICENSE.txt
  ../ice-palette/LICENSE.txt

Sound-board and generator code retain the repository's MIT license.
