Boundstoy explosion and buildup listening sketches

airy-puff-blast.wav is also the approved gameplay explosion sound.
Its use in gameplay does not change the CC0 license below.

All sixteen WAV files in this directory are original Boundstoy sound designs,
dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No third-party samples, recordings, instrument banks, themes or compositions
are used. Sounds are generated offline by seeded, original mathematical
synthesis. No attribution is required for the sound files.

Editable source in this repository:
  data/explosion-palette.ts
  scripts/prepare-explosion-palette.ts

Rebuild with Node 24+ and FFmpeg:
  node scripts/prepare-explosion-palette.ts

The manifest records source hashes, mastering settings, per-file hashes and
measured loudness/headroom. Measurement uses identical repeated-event trains;
it does not claim integrated LUFS of an isolated 225 ms clip.

Listening-page and tool code remain under the repository's MIT license.
CC0 covers the generated sound assets; it does not relicense FFmpeg.
